Privacy & credits

PocketPDF is a free document scanner made by BigPharmi. This page explains what happens to your scans and lists the open-source software the app is built with.

Your scans stay on your phone

Cropping, cleaning up and turning pages into a PDF all happen inside your browser, on your own device. Your photos, pages and PDFs are never uploaded or sent to anyone, including BigPharmi.

There is no account, no sign-in, no tracking, no analytics, no ads and no cookies.

What is saved on this phone

  • Your scanned pages, so they are still there if you close the app. They stay until you tap Clear all or remove a page. Anyone who uses this phone and opens PocketPDF can see them, so clear sensitive pages when you’re done.
  • Your PDF settings (page size, margins, quality, auto-clean).
  • A copy of the app itself, so it works offline.

PDFs you share or download are handled by your phone like any other file; PocketPDF keeps no copy of them.

What connects to the internet

  • GitHub Pages delivers the app’s files. Like any website host, GitHub can see your internet address when the app downloads; see the GitHub privacy statement.
  • jsDelivr is used only as a backup source for the auto-crop engine, if GitHub’s copy can’t be loaded.

Neither of them ever receives your photos or documents.

Removing everything

Tap Clear all in the app to remove every page. To remove the app’s saved copy and settings too, delete this site’s data in your browser settings.

On iPhone, Safari may clear the pages saved in the app if it isn’t used for about a week. Adding PocketPDF to your Home Screen avoids this. PDFs you have already saved are not affected.
